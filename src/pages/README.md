# Cloud Comfort Ride — Wix Velo Pages

This folder contains the Velo page scripts for the Cloud Comfort Ride website (`https://cloud-comfort-ride-flame.vercel.app/`):

- `masterPage.js` — Global header (`#ccrHeader`) and footer (`#ccrFooter`) navigation wiring.
- `Home.c1dmp.js` — Home page (`/`), wired to `#ccrHome`.
- `Services.js` — Specialized Medical Transit (`/services`), wired to `#ccrServices`.
- `About Us.drkx6.js` — About Us (`/about`), wired to `#ccrAbout`.
- `Why Cloud Medical.it7h9.js` — Driver Safety & Fleet (`/safety-drivers`), wired to `#ccrSafety`.
- `Book A Ride.js` — Online Booking Portal (`/book-a-ride`), wired to `#ccrBookRide`.
- `My Bookings.js` — Passenger Trip Portal & Vouchers (`/my-bookings`), wired to `#ccrMyBookings`.
- `Contact.js` — Dispatch & Contact Form (`/contact`), wired to `#ccrContact`.
- `Privacy Policy.aztx0.js` — Privacy Policy (`/privacy`), wired to `#ccrPrivacy`.
- `Terms of Service.tqouh.js` — Terms of Service (`/terms`), wired to `#ccrTerms`.
- `Accessibility Statement.js` — Accessibility Statement (`/accessibility`), wired to `#ccrAccessibility`.

> **Wix Studio Note:** When you add `Services`, `Book A Ride`, `My Bookings`, `Contact`, and `Accessibility Statement` in the Wix Studio Editor, Wix appends its generated 5-character page IDs (e.g. `Services.<id>.js`) and syncs them to this folder. Copy the corresponding wiring into the synced page file if Wix generates a new ID filename.
